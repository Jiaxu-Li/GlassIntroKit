# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

GlassIntroKit is a small project, and we want it to be a place where people can ask questions, report problems, and share work without being made to feel unwelcome. This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).

Everyone who takes part in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space run for the project is expected to:

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to Swift, Metal, or open source.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and accept feedback the same way.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear.
- Stay on topic, and keep discussion useful to the project.

The following are not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning comments, including about a person's identity, background, or beliefs.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or imagery.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or spam.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, and in public places when someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. They may edit or remove comments, close or lock issues and pull requests, and ban people from the project, temporarily or permanently, for behavior that breaks it. They will explain their decision when it is appropriate to do so.

To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment, issue, or pull request, or contact the maintainer, Jiaxu Li, through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Jiaxu-Li). Reports are handled privately, and the maintainer will not share the reporter's identity without consent.

If the maintainer is the subject of a report, use GitHub's "Report content" option, which lets GitHub review it.
